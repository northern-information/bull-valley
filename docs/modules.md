# Module map

Strict TypeScript throughout. The map (`public/data/bull-valley/`: `geo.json` and the `terrain.png` heightmap) is the game's own, committed and edited by hand; nothing fetches or regenerates it. Shapes that cross modules (world points and heights, geo.json, pack grid entries, the item types the drawing modules share) live in `src/interfaces.ts`; types one module owns stay in that module (the `Item` union is `items.ts`'s). Every module under `src/` and `worker/` has its line below: `tests/unit/modulemap.test.ts` fails on one the map does not name, and on a name with no module.

- `src/interfaces.ts` — shared types only, no runtime code
- `src/toml.d.ts` — the type of a Vite `?raw` import, for `copy.ts`'s `COPY.toml` and `laptopreadme.ts`'s `LAPTOP.TXT`
- `COPY.toml` (repo root) — every player-facing line, each with `text` and `by` (`"ai"` or `"tyler"`); `{name}` placeholders. `src/copy.ts` loads it (Vite `?raw`, so it ships in the client and the Worker) and exports `copy(key, vars)`; `src/copybook.ts` is the pure parsing and filling, shared with `tests/e2e/copy.ts`, which reads the file from disk. `tests/unit/copy.test.ts` fails on a key the code asks for that the file lacks, and on an entry nothing asks for. Modules the e2e specs import (`bindings.ts`, `account.ts`) must not import `copy.ts`: Playwright cannot load `?raw`
- `src/protocol.ts` — pure: the wire protocol, imported by the client and the Worker (frames, close codes, the shown-name rules the stamped username must pass, `parseClientMessage`)
- `src/sharedworld.ts` — pure: the shared world's rules as a reducer over the valley (rule 1: the persistent world, 2: where a raider comes back, 3: Marx's truck, 4: pickups, 5: the whistle, 6: the day's turn, 7: the shelves and the wallet, 8: the berry bushes, 9: Gron, 10: the pack, 11: the shadowmen, stepped in `shadowstep.ts`, 12: drops, 13: the Caretaker, 14: Moab's trade, 15: the season, 16: the daily task, 17: the tombstones, 18: corpse runs, 19: the stash, 20: the Book of Shadows, 21: friends and whispers, 22: the raider's level, 23: the Cabbage Stand, 24: health, 25: Erwin von Dutch's barter, 26: quests, 27: buffs, 28: Citgos and Citgo portals, 29: Bull Valley Dial-Up, 30: idle expeditions, 31: the laptop in the Citgo bathroom), and `wakeAt`, when the valley next needs waking
- `src/shadowstep.ts` — pure: rule 11's step over the reducer's `Valley`: the `Shadows` the valley keeps in memory, the raiders they cross round (`shadowRaiders`), Marx's headlights (`seeHeadlights`, `headlightsAt`), `stepShadows` (the shadowmen, the Caretaker and the tunnel shades, what they credit and the grace a strike leaves), each raider's share of its frame (`shadowmenNear`), what the step reads of where raiders stand (`forecourtMends`, `citgoVisits`), and the dev server's placements; `tests/unit/sharedworld.test.ts` pins it with the reducer
- `src/marx.ts` — pure: Matthew Marx's day as a chain of legs stamped with server times (parked and reading, donuts, the joyride, a charter to where the first one in picked, called, the ferry home): `settleTruck` moves it on through what is due, `board`, `charter`, `hopOut`, `call`, `arrive` (home from his donuts for an arrival) and `leave` change it, `nextChange` says when it moves on its own, and `TO_DOOR` is his walk from the tailgate to his door. The valley runs it; tune it in `CONFIG.truck`
- `src/truckradio.ts` — pure: Matthew Marx's radio, Day of Justice out of the cab while the truck rolls (`RADIO_URL`, streamed from assets.the-rn.info, never in the bundle or the repo): how it sounds from where the raider is (`radioMix`: as cut in the bed; out of it an inverse fall-off and a low-pass sliding down to `floorHz` by `far`, so from the Citgo only the lows carry) and where in the song the engine's clock is (`radioOffset`). Tune it in `CONFIG.truck.radio`. `src/truckradiorig.ts` plays it: its own `AudioContext` from the first frame after Begin, the song decoded once and looped through a low-pass and a gain while `Truck.engineSeconds` says the truck rolls, at the Music setting; none under e2e
- `src/donutwake.ts` — pure: what Marx's donuts leave: the rear wheels (`rearWheels`), the clods of grass and dirt they throw (`clodsDue`, `throwClod`, `stepClods`), the ruts along the leg's route up to where the truck has got to, so a client arriving mid-leg sees what is cut already (`tracksAlong`), and the field they tear (`inField`, never the road). Scenery, nothing on the wire. `src/donutwakerig.ts` draws it: the clods as one cloud of points, the ruts on a canvas sheet draped over the field that heals slowly. Tune it in `CONFIG.truck.donuts.spray` and `ruts`
- `src/truckplan.ts` — pure: what a client draws for a leg (`planLeg`: the road, the speed, from when, whether Marx walks to his door first, whether the drive ends parked at home), where a leg cut short left the truck (`approachStart`), the joyride out and home (`joyrideRoute`) and how long it takes (`joyrideMs`, for the hello)
- `src/worldsync.ts` — pure: the client's reading of a `WorldWire` snapshot: `settledBy` whether a `taken`, `bought`, `dropped` or `drop-taken` snapshot went to this raider; `aboard`, `seatOf` the bed seat, `boardable` whether the truck is ours to climb into, `countdown` (and `clockText`) the seconds until Marx leaves, `newLeg` whether the truck has a new leg to drive
- `src/cosmetics.ts` — pure: the cosmetics a raider can wear over a character (the Flaming Halo), each priced in pack items, what Moab offers a raider (`moabOffer`: the first he lacks and can pay for), and what a trade just gave the account to wear (`gainedOf`). `assets.ts` `buildFlamingHalo` draws the halo and `figure.ts` `attachCosmetics` puts it over the head; peers, Gron's turntable and Akashic (`halo-worn`) wear it
- `src/caretaker.ts` — pure: the Caretaker (sharedworld rule 13), in the maze's own metres: the paths it can walk (`mazeMap`, the cells the heart reaches), line of sight and passing room against the corn (`clearBetween`), the way down the paths (`routeTo`), and `stepCaretaker` (patrol out and home by turns, hunt, the windup and the lunge, the two-beam burn, forming again). Tune it in `CONFIG.caretaker`
- `src/caretakerrig.ts` — the Caretaker as seen (`assets.ts` `buildCaretaker` on `ground.at`, turned to the player): the valley's, drawn a beat behind the present from the shadowmen frames; its lantern drawn back flaring over the windup and swung out as it lunges (`CaretakerRig.setStrike`); it hands `loop.ts` its strike, where it was burned, and how far it floats from the player (the Book of Shadows)
- `src/book.ts` — pure: the Book of Shadows (sharedworld rule 20): `BOOK`, every entry in its chapter (`CHAPTERS`: places, shadows, folk, items; an item's words are its own, the rest `COPY.toml` `[book.<id>]` or `[outfits]`), `newlyFound` (what an ask adds: real entries not known, once each), `tallyOf`, the places a raider is within reach of (`Sight`, `sightsInReach`; `world.ts` places them as `world.sights`, tuned in `CONFIG.book.reach`) the items a pack holds (`itemsHeld`), and pages written as the log says them (`foundLines`, `bountyLine`)
- `src/bookhud.ts` — the Book of Shadows as read: the dialog `B` opens (tabs with each chapter's tally, the chapter's entries down the left, the open page on the right) and the toast that names what was just written (`announce`). `hud.ts` builds it as `hud.book`; `actions.ts` opens it and turns its pages
- `src/bookportraits.ts` — what each page shows turning (`portraitOf`): the valley's own builders (a pump for the Citgo, Marx's truck for the donut field, the figures for the folk), an item's pickup for an item; `itemthumbs.ts` `spinModel` draws it, dark until found
- `src/goal.ts` — pure: a goal an account works toward, the counting the season and the daily task share: `advance` (one more credit, and the reward on the one that reaches the goal, paid once), `shownProgress` for a tracker, `goalNews`
- `src/season.ts` — pure: the season (sharedworld rule 15): `SEASON` (Season One, The Caretaker: the goal and the reward), `tally` (one more burning, and the reward when it reaches the goal, paid once), `shownKills`, `newsOf`, `seasonOf`, a season frame read when it is this season's, and `seasonLine`, a burning as the log says it (both apps)
- `src/dailytask.ts` — pure: the daily task (sharedworld rule 16): `DAILY_TASK` (burn 5 shadows, $5), `tallyTask` (one more burn on a Central day, the count starting over when the day turns, and the reward when it reaches the goal, paid once a day), `onDay`, `shownCount`, `taskNews`, `taskOf`, a task frame read when it is this task's, and `taskLine`, a burn as the log says it (both apps)
- `src/friends.ts` — pure: friends and whispers (sharedworld rule 21): what asking comes to (`askOutcome`, `FRIENDS_MAX`), names in any case (`sameName`), roughly where a raider is (`whereabouts`), the list as the log says it (`friendLines`), and the client's reading of the valley's answers (`settledAsk`, `listAnswer` for a new list, `friendRefusal`, `whisperRefusal`, `friendNews`), shared by both apps
- `src/taskhud.ts` — the daily task as seen: its row under the season in the upper-right tracker (which shows only for a few seconds after the season, the daily task or the XP goes up, `SeasonHud.flash`), pips lit for today's burns, Done once paid
- `src/progression.ts` — pure: the raider's level (sharedworld rule 22): `XP` (what each `XpSource` grants, every grant in one table; a later track adds its source here), `xpToReach` and `levelOf` off the curve in `CONFIG.progression` (gentle: `base * (level - 1) ^ power` in all, uncapped by the season, never past `maxLevel`), `barOf` for the bar, `totals` (an account's grants summed) and `levelUp`
- `src/levelhud.ts` — the raider's level as seen: its row under the daily task in the upper-right tracker, the level and a bar filling toward the next. A new level sweeps a banner (`seasonhud.ts` `announce`) and adds a line to the log (`valleysync.ts`); peers wear theirs on the name tag (`peers.ts`), and `/online` names it
- `src/seasonhud.ts` — the season as seen: the tracker in the upper right, the card `hud.ts` puts on the intro and pause overlay, and the banners (`announce`, one at a time; `live`, `burned`)
- `src/corpses.ts` — pure: corpse runs (sharedworld rule 18): what a fall leaves on the body (`fallen`, never a key item), the pack it leaves (`emptied`, the key items kept), a body's key items split off it (`keysOff`), taking it back (`recover`), a body as the wire carries it (`corpseWire`, never what it holds), and the nearest of your own within reach (`nearestCorpse`). Tune the reach in `CONFIG.corpses`
- `src/corpsemeshes.ts` — the bodies in Three: one `figure.ts` body per corpse in its raider's outfit, on its back in the `sprawl` pose on `ground.at` with its raider's name tag over it (`peers.ts` `nameLabel`), kept in step with `GameState.corpses` (`sync`); `objectOf` hands the glow your own
- `src/stand.ts` — pure: the Cabbage Stand as each account's outpost (sharedworld rule 23): the ledger (`StandLedger`: level, the goods on the table, the cents banked by `since`), what it earns (`ratePerHour`, the level's flat rate for the share of the table filled), banks (`accruedAt` against the valley's clock, stopping at `capOf`) and when it fills (`fullAt`); putting goods out (`stock`), collecting (`collect`, the whole cents), the next level (`upgradePrice`, `affordsUpgrade`, `upgrade`), whether a raider stands at it for the valley (`atStand`), the wire check (`isStandLedger`), and the line for a refusal. Tune it in `CONFIG.stand`
- `src/npcdialog.ts` — the frame the stand's and Erwin's dialogs share (`styles.css` `.bv-npc`), which the pause overlay (`hud.ts`: the season, the controls, the raider and their settings, Begin in the footer) and the pack (`packhud.ts`: its header with the cash and the locker beside it, the item card and the right-click menu) wear too: the header (an eyebrow, the status on the right, the title, the keeper's line), numbered section heads, the footer with the valley's word and the way out (`bindings.ts` `DIALOG`), and square pips (`setPips`); each dialog lays its sections on the 12-column grid, and the wide ones (the intro, the stand, Erwin; `.bv-npc-ui--wide`) in two columns, the side section (`.bv-npc-side`) on the right, so each fits a 1280x720 window. No overlay scrolls itself or the page: each keeps a 24px gutter round its card, and a window too short for the card scrolls the card (`styles.css`, `.bv-account`, `.bv-intro`)
- `src/standdialog.ts` — the stand's dialog (E beside it), in three sections: the till (banked, earning, when it fills, Collect), the table (its slots, each good out and in the pack, the buttons that put them out), and the next level (what changes, the price against what you have, Upgrade saying what falls short, `stand.ts` `upgradeShortfall`); the ledger read live against the valley's clock every tick; `actions.ts` sends the frames and `valleysync.ts` applies the answers. `assets.ts` `buildStandDressing` dresses the stand for the raider's own level (`world.ts` `StandRig.setLevel`); `standart.ts` paints the canopy cloth and the OPEN board
- `src/laptop.ts` — pure: the laptop in the Citgo bathroom (sharedworld rule 31): a file's name, text and id as the wire takes them (`isFileName`, `isFileBody`, `isFileId`; `normalizeBody` keeps every space, tab and line and drops stray control characters), whether a save stands against the writer's own files (`saveRefusal`: `not-yours`, `name-taken`, `too-many`), whether a raider stands at a Citgo for the valley to open the files (`atLaptop`), the listing as one raider is sent it (`listingFor`, `mine` in place of the account) and folded into a folder a writer (`foldersOf`, the listener's first and always there), and the editor's `caretOf` and `expandTabs`. Tune it in `CONFIG.laptop`
- `LAPTOP.TXT` (repo root) — the laptop's readme, always its first file and never written over, shipped as typed (Vite `?raw`) so every space and line holds; `src/laptopreadme.ts` loads it (`README`)
- `src/laptopdialog.ts` — the laptop as used (E at the card table, through `actions.ts` `withDialog`): its screen fills the view in a dark bezel and opens on the editor (white on dark blue) with the readme up; Esc closes the editor onto the desktop (green hills under a blue sky, the readme, My Documents and Documents and Settings as icons, a window to browse every raider's folder with Up, Open, New Text Document and Delete, the taskbar with its start menu and clock), where anyone's file opens to read and the raider's own to write (Ctrl+S saves, a tab types a tab); Esc on the desktop or Turn Off Computer walks away, and the next sitting finds the editor and the readme again. It asks with the laptop frames and hears the answers through the `LaptopScreen` it hands back (`GameState.laptop`, fed by `valleysync.ts`); what raiders wrote goes in as text, never markup. Played alone only the readme is there
- `src/laptopart.ts` — canvas art for the laptop's screen as it stands with no one at it (`paintEditorScreen`: the editor's menu bar, the readme in its frame, the status bar)
- `src/laptopmodel.ts` — the laptop on the card table (`buildBathLaptop`): a thick charcoal machine, its lid up, the screen painted by `laptopart.ts` with a blue halo before it; `update` breathes the screen and sets the bathroom's light from it (`bathglow.ts` `setBathGlow`, out when the display is parked nowhere). `stationdisplay.ts` carries it with the shelf display to the nearest Citgo (`ShelfDisplay.laptop`, what the glow rings)
- `src/bathglow.ts` — the Citgo bathroom's light: `bathLit`, a material that takes none of the scene's lights (not the moon, the sky or the back room's tube) and is lit in its shader by the laptop's screen, a blue that falls off across the room (`CONFIG.laptop.glow`), and by this raider's own flashlight, its cone as the real one throws it (`setBathTorch`, from `fphands.ts` every frame; `CONFIG.flashlight.bathLevel`); every bathroom face wears it (`citgo.ts` the tile, porcelain, mirror, door, the card table and the linings `store.ts` lays inside the shared walls, and the WASH HANDS sign, `StoreSign.dark`), and `setBathGlow` places the one glow, so every bathroom but the nearest stands black. No Three light is added, so nothing recompiles and nothing leaks into the back room
- `src/stash.ts` — pure: the stash (sharedworld rule 19): whether a raider stands at a Citgo for the valley to open their locker (`atLocker`), how much one move takes (`moveAmount`, a drop's amount), and a move from one side to the other (`move`). Tune it in `CONFIG.stash`
- `src/drops.ts` — pure: dropped items (sharedworld rule 12), the dimes a burst shadowman leaves and a spider's $20 (`DIMES`, `TWENTY`, `isCash`, `centsOf`, `dimesFor`, rule 11), the Caretaker's gold bullion (`GOLD_BULLION`, rule 13), and what one step of the valley leaves lying (`spillsOf`): how much one drop takes (`dropAmount`: the open container or one of anything else, or the whole stack), where it lands (`dropSpot`: ahead of the raider, spread round a small circle by id; tune it in `CONFIG.drops`), and what taking it up leaves (`takeUp`). The valley's reducer runs it
- `src/math.ts` — pure: the little arithmetic more than one module reaches for (`clamp01`, `goldenSpot`: things spread round a circle by the golden angle, the drops' scatter and the tombstones' step)
- `src/names.ts` — pure: the shadowmen's folklore names, the Bull Valley Scaduscope's (`forgotten-industries` `src/assets/js/bull-valley-scaduscope/names.js`) word for word: `generateName`. Carved on the stones like trade dress, so not in `COPY.toml`
- `src/graves.ts` — pure: the tombstones burst shadowmen leave (sharedworld rule 17): `burialsOf` names the bursts of kind `man` (no other shadow is buried), `graveSpot` stands each a step off its dimes (`CONFIG.graves.offset`), `bury` lays them and lets the oldest go past `CONFIG.graves.max`. The valley's reducer runs it
- `src/graveart.ts` — canvas art for a tombstone's face: R.I.P., a cross, and the name cut in; `src/gravestones.ts` — the tombstones in Three, one `assets.ts` `buildTombstone` per grave on `ground.at`, kept in step with `GameState.graves` (`sync`)
- `src/waterside.ts` — pure: where the water's edges run, as the valley needs it for the spiders (`waterMapOf` from the survey's water, `nearWater`, `isWaterMap` for the hello)
- `src/billart.ts` — canvas art for the $20 bill a spider bursts into (`assets.ts` `buildTwenty`)
- `src/dropmeshes.ts` — the drops in Three: one `assets.ts` `buildPickup` mesh per drop on `ground.at` (`buildDimes` for dimes, `buildTwenty` for a spider's $20), kept in step with `GameState.drops` (`sync`), each handed back as a pickup (`DropPickup`), so E, the glow and the floating label treat a drop like any other; `loop.ts` passes them to the resolver with `world.pickups`
- `src/daily.ts` — pure: the daily clock behind the berry bush (the Central calendar day of an instant, the next midnight Central, DST included); the Worker runs it against its own clock
- `src/clock.ts` — pure: the server-clock offset from ping round trips
- `src/presence.ts` — pure: the peer table, each peer's last few seconds of states kept in a `framebuffer.ts` run and drawn between the two either side of the moment (`samplePeer`), and whether our own state is worth a frame (`stateChanged`)
- `src/net.ts` — the WebSocket client: a session refresh before each open, hello (or a `dial` for Bull Valley Dial-Up, `dial`), reconnect with backoff, pings; `ready` resolves online or offline (Dial-Up's modem reads it for NO CARRIER), a ping unanswered past `CONFIG.net.deadMs` drops a half-open line, and `onRefused` hears a 4xxx close
- `src/account.ts` — pure: the `/auth` protocol shared by the client and the Worker (providers, the username rule, wire shapes, redirects, the `?auth=` return flags)
- `src/cookies.ts` — pure: the session cookie names, parse and serialize
- `src/auth.ts` — the client's calls to `/auth` (who is signed in, refresh, confirm signup, username, sign out)
- `src/signin.ts` — the account step behind the main menu's Create Account (Back returns to the menu): the sign-in card, then Choose Your Username with the Magic Word field for a new raider. It and Gron's dialog word a username check the same way (`auth.ts` `availabilityOf`)
- `src/grondialog.ts` — talking to Gron (E beside him): change your username, and change your character and guitar finish on a small turntable preview. The pointer comes free while it is open and the game's keys stand aside; `actions.ts` applies the changes and tells the valley
- `src/accountpanel.ts` — the account panel, opened from the Account link on the select, the main menu's Settings, or the pause overlay: linked providers with Unlink (never the last), Link Another Account in a popup that lands on `public/auth-done.html` (which posts the result back and closes; a blocked popup falls back to a full-page round trip), and Sign Out. While it is open, the select's keys are its alone
- `worker/auth.ts` — the `/auth` routes and the identity stamp on the upgrade; `worker/oauth.ts` the provider flow, `worker/tokens.ts` the JWTs (`jose`), `worker/accounts.ts` the store interface and an in-memory store for tests, `worker/d1accounts.ts` the D1 store (the look, the hotbar, the settings and the friendships too), `worker/packs.ts` the pack store interface and an in-memory store, `worker/d1packs.ts` the D1 pack store (the season's and the daily task's progress and the locker too, the Book of Shadows, the Cabbage Stand and the expedition out; `welcome` reads all of it in one batch), `worker/env.ts` the secrets and origins, `worker/ratelimit.ts` the rate limits (two bindings in `wrangler.jsonc`: strict at 10 a minute for the routes that start a sign-in or change an account, loose at 60 for the username check, the session refresh, the look, the hotbar and the settings; keyed by account, then pending signup, then client address; the dev provider, `/auth/me`, sign-out and the callback are never limited; a limiter error lets the request through)
- `src/peers.ts` — the other players in Three: one `figure.ts` body per peer and a pixelated name sprite
- `worker/index.ts` — the Worker router (`/ws` to the valley, everything else to the assets binding, the dev stamp); `worker/ValleyDO.ts` — the Durable Object: sockets, storage, the alarm, the reducer, and the shadowmen's clock (`startTicker`, which the Worker tests replace to step by hand); it reaches D1 through `accounts()` and `packs()`, which the Worker tests replace with the memory stores. The hello and the `appearance` frame accept only the select's roster (`characters.ts` `isSelectable`), never an NPC's outfit. Checked by `worker/tsconfig.json` with Workers types, so the Worker tests live in `tests/worker/`, not `tests/unit/`; `worker/www.ts` — the www redirect, a Worker of its own (`wrangler.www.jsonc`)

- `src/entry.ts` — the page's one script (`index.html`): `dialpick.ts` `pickApp` says which app the page opens, and it loads that one alone by dynamic import, `main.ts` for the 3D valley or `dialmain.ts` for Bull Valley Dial-Up, so a phone fetches three.js only once Dial-Up wants its models (`dial3d.ts`). The 3D valley therefore loads after the page's load event; the e2e fixtures wait for the colophon's hint before pressing Space (`toMenu`)
- `src/dialpick.ts` — pure: which app a page opens (`pickApp`: `?dialup` or `?valley3d` say outright; otherwise a device that only touches, a coarse pointer and no fine one, dials up) and the same page in the other app (`switchUrl`)
- `src/build.ts` — pure: the build's name (`buildLabel`: the protocol, the UTC day it was built, `buildDay`, and the commit, as `v38.20261020.398c471`); `vite.config.ts` names the day and the commit (`define`). `src/buildtag.ts` shows it (`Build …`) on every screen: in the 3D valley in the lower right corner, the titles included (on the main menu under its credit), and on Dial-Up on a strip of its own at the very bottom, under the tabs (`dialup.css` `--dial-build`)
- `src/fonts.ts` — `fontsReady`, the wait on IBM Plex Mono both boots make before any text exists
- `src/preload.ts` — `imagesReady`, the wait both boots make beside the fonts on the film grain every title card and the modem lie under (`GRAIN_URL`, preloaded by `index.html`) and the logos, so none pops in
- `src/main.ts` — boot: loads the survey, builds the scene and the systems into one `Game`, and hands it to the modules below. Game rules go in the pure modules, never here or in them
- `src/game.ts` — the `Game` boot builds (the systems, the truck's roads and what the valley is told of them) and the `GameState` they share (the shared world, the truck alone and the leg it drives, the bed, the pack, the wallet, the pending asks, the item effects)
- `src/dialmain.ts` — Bull Valley Dial-Up's boot (sharedworld rule 29): the brand (`document.title`, `public/dialup.webmanifest`, the `bv-dialup` class `src/dialup.css` keys on), the modem, then `NetClient.dial` (a `dial` instead of a hello), the sign-in card for a phone no one is signed in on (`signin.ts`, a full-page round trip that comes back to the modem), and the shell fed by the valley's frames through `dialstate.ts`; the stand's dialog is the 3D valley's own (`standdialog.ts`), its frames sent from here
- `src/modem.ts` — pure: Dial-Up's modem, the splash every launch opens on: `SCRIPT` (the terminal's banner first, the build's name and the 3D valley's epigraph, `COPY.toml` `[titles]`, then the handshake's lines and cues on its clock, `HANDSHAKE_MS`), a tap that dials and a second that skips (`tap`), how the line answers (`connected` once the valley's welcome comes, `signedOut` for LOGIN, `noCarrier` for NO CARRIER and a redial), and `view`, what is printed and heard at a moment and when it gives way (`HOLD_MS` after CONNECT). Its words are `COPY.toml` `[dialup.modem]`
- `src/modemaudio.ts` — the modem heard: a 56k handshake synthesized in Web Audio (dial tone, the number as DTMF, the ring, the 2100 Hz answer and the bong, training's noise and chirps, the hush on connect), never a recording; built on the tap that dials, none under e2e
