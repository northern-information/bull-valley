# Module map

Strict TypeScript throughout. The map (`public/data/bull-valley/`: `geo.json` and the `terrain.png` heightmap) is the game's own, committed and edited by hand; nothing fetches or regenerates it. Shapes that cross modules (world points and heights, geo.json, pack grid entries, the item types the drawing modules share) live in `src/interfaces.ts`; types one module owns stay in that module (the `Item` union is `items.ts`'s). Every module under `src/` and `worker/` has its line below: `tests/unit/modulemap.test.ts` fails on one the map does not name, and on a name with no module.

- `src/interfaces.ts` — shared types only, no runtime code
- `src/toml.d.ts` — the type of a Vite `?raw` import, for `copy.ts`'s `COPY.toml` and `laptopreadme.ts`'s `LAPTOP.TXT`
- `COPY.toml` (repo root) — every player-facing line, each with `text` and `by` (`"ai"` or `"tyler"`); `{name}` placeholders. `src/copy.ts` loads it (Vite `?raw`, so it ships in the client and the Worker) and exports `copy(key, vars)`; `src/copybook.ts` is the pure parsing and filling, shared with `tests/e2e/copy.ts`, which reads the file from disk. `tests/unit/copy.test.ts` fails on a key the code asks for that the file lacks, and on an entry nothing asks for. Modules the e2e specs import (`bindings.ts`, `account.ts`) must not import `copy.ts`: Playwright cannot load `?raw`
- `src/protocol.ts` — pure: the wire protocol, imported by the client and the Worker (frames, close codes, the shown-name rules the stamped username must pass, `parseClientMessage`)
- `src/sharedworld.ts` — pure: the shared world's rules as a reducer over the valley (rule 1: the persistent world, 2: where a raider comes back, 3: Marx's truck, 4: pickups, 5: the whistle, 6: the day's turn, 7: the shelves and the wallet, 8: the berry bushes, 9: names and characters, 10: the pack, 11: the shadowmen, stepped in `shadowstep.ts`, 12: drops, 13: the Caretaker, 14: Moab's trade, 15: the season, 16: the daily task, 17: the tombstones, 18: corpse runs, 19: the stash, 20: the Book of Shadows, 21: friends and whispers, 22: the raider's level, 23: the Cabbage Stand, 24: health, 25: Erwin von Dutch's barter, 26: quests, 27: buffs, 28: Citgos and Citgo portals, 29: Bull Valley Dial-Up, 30: idle expeditions, 31: the laptop in the Citgo bathroom, 32: empties, 33: the Recycling Center, 34: parties, 35: crews, 36: fishing and the firefly pond, 37: waypoints, 38: the kit, 39: the topiaries and the torch, 40: the campfire, 41: the drive-in reel, 42: the celestial graveyard and the mechs), and `wakeAt`, when the valley next needs waking
- `src/shadowstep.ts` — pure: rule 11's step over the reducer's `Valley`: the `Shadows` the valley keeps in memory, the raiders they cross round, with the height of their feet (`shadowRaiders`), Marx's headlights (`seeHeadlights`, `headlightsAt`), `stepShadows` (the shadowmen, the Caretaker and the tunnel shades, what they credit and the grace a strike leaves), each raider's share of its frame (`shadowmenNear`), what the step reads of where raiders stand (`forecourtMends`, `citgoVisits`, the payphones raiders stand at that their accounts have not found (`phoneVisits`)), and the dev server's placements; `tests/unit/sharedworld.test.ts` pins it with the reducer
- `src/marx.ts` — pure: Matthew Marx's day as a chain of legs stamped with server times (parked and reading, donuts, the joyride, a charter to where the first one in picked, called, the ferry home): `settleTruck` moves it on through what is due, `board`, `charter`, `hopOut`, `call`, `arrive` (home from his donuts for an arrival) and `leave` change it, `nextChange` says when it moves on its own, and `TO_DOOR` is his walk from the tailgate to his door. The valley runs it; tune it in `CONFIG.truck`
- `src/truckradio.ts` — pure: Matthew Marx's radio, Day of Justice out of the cab while the truck rolls (`RADIO_URL`, streamed from assets.the-rn.info, never in the bundle or the repo): how it sounds from where the raider is (`radioMix`: as cut in the bed; out of it an inverse fall-off and a low-pass sliding down to `floorHz` by `far`, so from the Citgo only the lows carry) and where in the song the engine's clock is (`radioOffset`). Tune it in `CONFIG.truck.radio`. `src/truckradiorig.ts` plays it: its own `AudioContext` from the first frame after Begin, the song decoded once and looped through a low-pass and a gain while `Truck.engineSeconds` says the truck rolls, at the Music setting; none under e2e
- `src/donutwake.ts` — pure: what Marx's donuts leave: the rear wheels (`rearWheels`), the clods of grass and dirt they throw (`clodsDue`, `throwClod`, `stepClods`), the ruts along the leg's route up to where the truck has got to, so a client arriving mid-leg sees what is cut already (`tracksAlong`), and the field they tear (`inField`, never the road). Scenery, nothing on the wire. `src/donutwakerig.ts` draws it: the clods as one cloud of points, the ruts on a canvas sheet draped over the field that heals slowly. Tune it in `CONFIG.truck.donuts.spray` and `ruts`
- `src/truckplan.ts` — pure: what a client draws for a leg (`planLeg`: the road, the speed, from when, whether Marx walks to his door first, whether the drive ends parked at home), where a leg cut short left the truck (`approachStart`), the joyride out and home (`joyrideRoute`) and how long it takes (`joyrideMs`, for the hello)
- `src/worldsync.ts` — pure: the client's reading of a `WorldWire` snapshot, and of a pack frame (`giftsIn`, a gift the valley gave, sharedworld rule 37; `toolsIn`, a kind newly in the pack the hand has a use for, which goes onto the bar): `settledBy` whether a `taken`, `bought`, `dropped` or `drop-taken` snapshot went to this raider; `aboard`, `seatOf` the bed seat, `boardable` whether the truck is ours to climb into, `countdown` (and `clockText`) the seconds until Marx leaves, `newLeg` whether the truck has a new leg to drive; `myBodies` the raider's own bodies not yet being taken back, for E and the map
- `src/cosmetics.ts` — pure: the cosmetics a raider can wear over a character (the Flaming Halo), each priced in pack items, what Moab offers a raider (`moabOffer`: the first he lacks and can pay for), what gold counts for in his trade window (`moabWorth`: one an ounce), and what a trade just gave the account to wear (`gainedOf`). `assets.ts` `buildFlamingHalo` draws the halo and `figure.ts` `attachCosmetics` puts it over the head; peers, Moab's Your Character turntable and Akashic (`halo-worn`) wear it
- `src/caretaker.ts` — pure: the Caretaker (sharedworld rule 13), in the maze's own metres: the paths it can walk (`mazeMap`, the cells the heart reaches), line of sight and passing room against the corn (`clearBetween`), the way down the paths (`routeTo`), and `stepCaretaker` (patrol out and home by turns, hunt, the windup and the lunge, the burn under two raiders' lights, a beam or a torch held close, forming again). Tune it in `CONFIG.caretaker`
- `src/caretakerrig.ts` — the Caretaker as seen (`assets.ts` `buildCaretaker` on `ground.at`, turned to the player): the valley's, drawn a beat behind the present from the shadowmen frames; its lantern drawn back flaring over the windup and swung out as it lunges (`CaretakerRig.setStrike`); it hands `loop.ts` its strike, where it was burned, and how far it floats from the player (the Book of Shadows)
- `src/topiaries.ts` — pure: the topiaries, the Overlook's hedge animals in the corn maze (`TOPIARIES`: two lions, a dog, a rabbit, a buffalo), in the maze's own metres: where one may stand (`topiarySpots`, the middle of a corridor along the road, its corn either side, off the heart, the gate and each other), each animal's own share of them (`dealSpots`), where each should stand on the valley's clock (`topiariesAt`, a slot each, staggered; nothing on the wire), whether one a raider sees goes there (`mayMove`: only unseen at both ends and never onto the raider), and the corridor's gate on the walls (`topiaryGate`, `isTopiaryGate`, `spotOfGate`); and the fire (sharedworld rule 39): each one's health as the valley keeps it (`TopiaryHealth`, `freshTopiaries`, `burnedAway`), a torch's bite on the valley's roll (`torchDamage`), a blow and burning away (`hitTopiary`), growing back (`regrow`, `nextRegrowth`), whether a raider could have reached one (`inTorchReach`, `spineDistance`), which one a swing lands on (`swingTarget`), a grown-back one showing itself only unseen (`mayAppear`), and its death (`deathPose`: the fire, the roll onto its back, the kicking, the char and the burn down into the ground over `CONFIG.topiaries.deathSeconds`); `theTopiaries` the game's spots and pools, worked out once. Not shadows: no beam touches them, and only a torch's blow touches them. `src/topiarymodel.ts` is the animals (`buildTopiary`, `buildTopiaryParts`: clipped lumps of two greens, scaled nose to tail to fill a corridor, a roll pivot along the spine and each leg on its own hip; `poseDeath` plays a `deathPose` on them, the fire, the roll, the kicking legs and the char), and `src/topiaryrig.ts` places them (`placeTopiaries`, from `cornmaze.ts`: every spot's corridor a gate on `Walls` that holds while an animal stands in it, `loop.ts` asking it through `setGates`; each animal on `ground.at`, turned broadside) and steps them on the valley's clock with the valley's word on their health (`update`: the bar over a hurt one, the fire as one burns away and its corridor opening, `struck` for a blow's shudder, `target` for a swing; the camera's frustum, `CONFIG.topiaries.sightRange` and the corn, `Walls.between`, unless up the lookout, decide what this raider sees; a `topiary-rustle` where one lands within earshot). Akashic shows `topiary-lion`, `-dog`, `-rabbit` and `-buffalo`, and `topiary-dying`, a lion's death on a loop. Tune them in `CONFIG.topiaries`
- `src/torchmodel.ts` — the torch (`buildTorch`: a pine stake, a kerosene rag wired round its head, and its fire, `fire.ts` `buildFlames`, playing on its own through `setMotion`) and the red toolbox at the lookout's foot it comes out of (`buildToolbox`, the spares standing up out of it); `cornmaze.ts` places the toolbox, `pickups.ts` makes the torch the pack's, and `heldmodels.ts` `buildHeldTorch` puts it in a fist. Akashic shows `torch` and `toolbox`
- `src/torchhand.ts` — the torch in the raider's own hand (`TorchHand`), drawn as the phone is (`phone.ts`'s hand, palette and `fillPolys`): the fist round the stake, the rag and its licking fire and sparks in one pixelated canvas (`.bv-torch`), up from the bottom right while the torch is in hand, tipped and carried across the view by a swing (`held.ts` `swingPose`); `loop.ts` steps it beside the hands
- `src/healthbar.ts` — the Elden Ring health bar on a small pixelated sprite (`buildHealthBar`, `drawHealthBar`, `setHealthBar`: a loss at once, `stepHealthBar`: a gain climbed to, `disposeHealthBar`): a peer wears one under their name (`peers.ts`), and every enemy one over it once hurt (`enemybars.ts`)
- `src/enemybars.ts` — the health bar over every enemy (`EnemyBars`: `show` each one a frame, `sweep` the gone; `bodyHeight` how tall a body stands): the shadowmen, spiders and spiderlings (`shadowbodies.ts`), the Caretaker (`caretakerrig.ts`), the tunnel shades and the Warden (`tunnelrig.ts`) and the topiaries (`topiaryrig.ts`), each a sprite of its own over the body so it holds still while the body shakes and flickers, shown once hurt and within `CONFIG.healthBars.range` (`shadowsync.ts` `barShown`), sized to the body (`barWidth`), filled by `shadeHealth` (or a topiary's kept hp)
